# Lark Corporate Contributor License Agreement (CCLA)

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Lark, a project of **Bag of
Holding, Inc.** ("the Company"). This Corporate CLA lets an organization
("Corporation") contribute on behalf of its employees.

This agreement covers Contributions Submitted to the Company by the Corporation
and by the employees it designates. An authorized representative of the
Corporation should complete the details below, sign, and return it to
**team@lark.sh**. Designated employees should additionally sign the
[Individual CLA](individual-cla.md).

## 1. Definitions

**"Corporation"** means the entity identified in the signature block below and any
of its subsidiaries that it controls.

**"Contribution"**, **"Submit"**, and **"Work"** have the same meanings as in the
[Individual CLA](individual-cla.md).

**"Designated Employees"** means the employees and agents of the Corporation
listed in Schedule A (as amended from time to time) who are authorized to Submit
Contributions on behalf of the Corporation.

Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Corporation grants to
the Company and to recipients of software distributed by the Company a perpetual,
worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license
to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform,
**sublicense, relicense, and distribute** Contributions made by the Corporation
and its Designated Employees, and such derivative works, **under any license
terms, including proprietary/commercial license terms.**

## 3. Grant of Patent License

Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Corporation grants to
the Company and to recipients of software distributed by the Company a perpetual,
worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable (except as stated
below) patent license to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and
otherwise transfer the Work, where such license applies only to those patent
claims licensable by the Corporation that are necessarily infringed by a
Contribution alone or by combination of the Contribution with the Work to which it
was Submitted.

If any entity institutes patent litigation alleging that a Contribution, or the
Work to which it was contributed, constitutes direct or contributory patent
infringement, any patent licenses granted to that entity under this Agreement for
that Contribution or Work shall terminate as of the date such litigation is filed.

## 4. Representations

The Corporation represents that:

1. Each Contribution it Submits, and each Contribution Submitted by its Designated
   Employees, is authorized and is the Corporation's original creation (or that
   third-party material is conspicuously identified per Section 5 of the ICLA).
2. It is legally entitled to grant the above licenses.
3. The persons listed in Schedule A are authorized to Submit Contributions on the
   Corporation's behalf, and the Corporation will maintain that list.

It is the Corporation's responsibility to notify the Company when any change is
required to the list of Designated Employees.
